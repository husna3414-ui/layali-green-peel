Informed consent

This informed consent form aims to ensure that you have comprehensive written information regarding the risks, benefits, alternatives, expected outcomes, recovery process and possible complications associated with a Bio-Microneedling Facial with Green Peel (Dr. Schrammek herbal peeling).

This document is intended to supplement your discussion with your practitioner. It is very important that you thoroughly read and understand all information presented. If you have any questions or uncertainties regarding the procedure, expected results, side effects, contraindications, aftercare or risks, you must discuss them with your practitioner before signing.

By signing this form, you confirm that you understand the nature of the treatment, the potential risks and complications, the alternatives available, and that results vary and cannot be guaranteed.

17

What is this treatment?

The Green Peel is a natural herbal skin-renewal treatment (Dr. Schrammek). A carefully balanced mixture of herbs, algae, enzymes, vitamins and minerals is massaged into the skin to activate the skin's own renewal process, encouraging the skin to shed tired surface layers and rebuild fresher, brighter, smoother skin.

Bio-microneedling uses a device with very fine needles to create tiny controlled micro-channels in the skin. This stimulates new collagen and helps the herbal actives absorb and work more effectively.

Different formula strengths exist — from lighter (Fresh-Up / Mild), with little or no visible peeling, to the deeper Classic formula, which produces visible peeling over roughly 2–5 days. The right formula for you will be agreed at your consultation.

18

Intended benefits

The aim of a Bio-Microneedling Facial with Green Peel is to renew and refresh the skin. Intended benefits may include:

  • Brighter, fresher-looking skin
  • A more even skin tone and softening of pigmentation and sun damage
  • Smoother, refined skin texture
  • Softening of fine lines
  • Collagen stimulation and improved overall skin quality and radiance

This is a skin-renewal facial treatment. It works on the quality and surface of the skin — it cannot lift or volumise the face and cannot remove deep or established scarring.

The level of change depends on your starting skin, the formula strength used, how many sessions you have and how well you follow your aftercare. Results build gradually over days to weeks as the skin peels and renews, and are best assessed once the peeling has fully settled.

For most people a course of treatments gives the best result, followed by maintenance treatments to keep it. Individual results vary and no specific result can be guaranteed.

Risks & complications

While every effort is made to minimise risks, no treatment is entirely risk-free. It has been explained to me that risks include, but are not limited to:

Common

  • Discomfort during and after treatment
  • Redness
  • Peeling & flaking (particularly with the Classic formula) — this may continue for up to 14 days
  • Itching or irritation
  • Tightness & dryness
  • Sensitivity & tenderness
  • Mild swelling
  • Pinpoint bleeding or mild bruising from microneedling

Less common

  • Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (darkening) — the most important risk, and higher in darker skin types (Fitzpatrick IV–VI). It may be temporary or, occasionally, longer-lasting.
  • Post-inflammatory hypopigmentation (lightening)
  • Temporary acne breakouts or spots as the skin renews
  • Infection (bacterial)
  • Reactivation of the cold-sore / herpes simplex virus
  • Prolonged redness or sensitivity
  • Allergic or hypersensitivity reaction
  • Delayed healing
  • Uneven or unsatisfactory result requiring further treatment

Rare but serious

  • Scarring — usually only if the skin is picked, scratched or not cared for
  • Severe or spreading infection
  • Severe allergic reaction
  • Permanent pigmentation change

Unforeseen risks may also occur. As with any procedure, it is not possible to list every complication, and rare or unexpected reactions can happen even when everything is done correctly.

Pigmentation: Because this treatment renews the skin, there is a real risk of temporary or, rarely, longer-lasting darkening — especially in darker skin tones. Strict sun avoidance and daily SPF 50 afterwards are essential, and I must not pick or peel the skin.

Emergency warning signs — act quickly

Contact Layali Clinic immediately on 07787 708200 if you develop:

  • Severe or rapidly increasing pain
  • White, grey, blue, purple or black skin
  • Blistering or skin breakdown
  • Rapidly increasing swelling
  • Spreading redness or heat
  • Pus or discharge
  • Fever or feeling severely unwell

Call 999 or go to A&E immediately if you develop:

  • Breathing or swallowing difficulty
  • Facial or throat swelling
  • Chest pain
  • Visual disturbance or loss of vision
  • Weakness, confusion, collapse or other neurological symptoms

Acting quickly matters — some complications are time-critical, so please do not wait. Out of hours, if you cannot reach the clinic, call NHS 111, or 999/attend A&E for anything severe or rapidly worsening.

Tell any other healthcare professional (A&E, GP, 111) the treatment you had, the product, the date and the injection/treatment site(s), and show them this consent so they can help you quickly.

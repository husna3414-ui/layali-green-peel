After Your Treatment

Bio Microneedling Facial Aftercare & Recovery

For professional sponge-spicule skin-renewal treatments.

Please follow these instructions carefully after your treatment. Your skin barrier will be temporarily more sensitive, so appropriate aftercare is important to reduce irritation, protect the skin and recognise any possible complication promptly.

Bio microneedling commonly uses microscopic sponge-derived spicules massaged or pressed into the upper layers of the skin. The spicules temporarily disrupt the skin barrier and may remain within the superficial skin for approximately 48–72 hours while the skin naturally sheds them. They differ from mechanical needles. The instructions given by your practitioner for the exact product and treatment strength take priority over this general guide.

Your recovery, at a glance

01
Day of treatment

Redness like mild sunburn, with warmth and tightness. Use only approved products and protect the skin from the sun.

02
First 24 hours

Use soothing/recovery products only. Avoid makeup, actives, heat and exercise.

03
Days 2–3

Spicules shed — expect prickling and mild flaking as the skin renews.

04
Days 3–7

Redness settles. Reintroduce gentle skincare and keep using SPF.

05
1–2 weeks

Skin looks fresher. Restart actives one at a time once fully settled.

Managing your symptoms at home

Most reactions are mild and settle on their own — here's how to stay comfortable and support healing:

Helps

  • Use only the soothing/recovery products we gave you for the first 24 hours, then a simple fragrance-free moisturiser
  • Cleanse with cool or lukewarm water using fingertips only, and pat dry
  • Let any flaking or peeling shed naturally
  • Daily broad-spectrum SPF 30+ once the surface is intact
  • Paracetamol if you need mild pain relief (if normally safe for you)
  • Keep hands, hair, pillowcases and your phone clean and off the skin

Skip

  • Picking, scratching, scrubbing or exfoliating
  • Retinol, acids, vitamin C and other actives for 5–7 days
  • Makeup for at least 24 hours
  • Heat, heavy sweating and swimming for ~72 hours
  • Direct sun and fake tan
  • Any at-home spicule product while healing
Redness like mild sunburn usually settles within 1–2 days and the prickly feeling eases as the spicules shed over 48–72 hours. Contact us if burning is intense, the skin blisters or weeps, redness spreads, or you feel unwell.

What is normal after treatment?

Normal

During the initial recovery period, it may be normal to experience:

Completely normal

  • Redness similar to mild sunburn
  • Warmth
  • Tightness
  • Mild swelling
  • Tenderness
  • Dryness
  • Itching
  • A prickling or tingling sensation
  • Sensitivity when touching or washing the skin
  • Mild stinging when skincare is applied
  • Temporary roughness
  • Mild flaking or peeling
  • Skin feeling dry or tight before shedding
  • Temporary darkening of existing pigmentation before it settles
  • Mild sensitivity lasting several days

A prickling sensation may be noticeable when the skin is touched, rubbed against clothing or pressed into a pillow, because the microscopic spicules can temporarily remain within the superficial skin. Do not repeatedly touch the skin to test the sensation. Redness and warmth should gradually improve.

Contact Layali Clinic if symptoms are severe, rapidly worsening or not improving as expected.

Immediately after treatment

Do

  • Keep the treated skin clean
  • Wash your hands before touching your face
  • Use only products supplied or approved by Layali Clinic
  • Keep hair away from treated skin
  • Use a clean pillowcase that evening
  • Drink water normally
  • Avoid unnecessary touching
  • Protect the skin from direct sunlight
  • Follow the product-specific instructions given to you

Do not

  • Rub, scratch or massage the skin
  • Pick at dry or peeling skin
  • Apply makeup immediately
  • Apply perfume, fragranced mist or fake tan
  • Use facial rollers or gua sha
  • Apply retinol, acids or exfoliants
  • Use unapproved serums
  • Apply ice directly to the skin
  • Use a cleansing brush or exfoliating cloth
  • Book another facial treatment while healing

During the first 24 hours

Use only the soothing or recovery product recommended by your practitioner.

Avoid

  • Makeup
  • Foundation or concealer
  • Strong cleanser
  • Retinol or tretinoin
  • Glycolic acid
  • Salicylic acid
  • Lactic acid
  • Exfoliating toner
  • Strong vitamin C
  • Benzoyl peroxide
  • Alcohol-based skincare
  • Fragranced skincare
  • Essential oils
  • Scrubs
  • Facial brushes
  • Self-tanning products
  • Unapproved sheet masks
  • Home microneedling or spicule products

Do not use another spicule-containing product at home while recovering.

Cleansing

Follow the exact washing time provided by your practitioner. When cleansing is permitted:

How to cleanse

  • Wash your hands first
  • Use cool or lukewarm water
  • Use a gentle non-foaming cleanser if approved
  • Use fingertips only
  • Do not scrub
  • Do not use a flannel, sponge or brush
  • Rinse gently
  • Pat dry with a clean soft towel
  • Do not drag or rub the skin

If water or cleanser causes intense burning, rinse gently and contact Layali Clinic.

Moisturiser & recovery products

After 24 hours, use a simple recovery moisturiser. Aftercare at this stage should focus on supporting the barrier, reducing dryness, limiting irritation, and protecting the skin from environmental exposure. Avoid introducing multiple new products at once.

Do not use

  • Strongly fragranced moisturiser
  • Essential oils
  • Exfoliating moisturiser
  • Retinoid cream
  • Lightening or bleaching products
  • Acne medication unless approved
  • Unprescribed steroid or antibiotic cream

Makeup

Avoid makeup for at least 24 hours, and longer if the skin is red, irritated, broken or sensitive. After a stronger treatment this may be up to 72 hours. When you restart makeup:

When restarting

  • Wash your hands
  • Use clean brushes and sponges
  • Do not share applicators
  • Apply gently
  • Avoid heavy or long-wear formulas initially
  • Remove gently
  • Do not apply over blistered, bleeding or damaged skin

Tingling & prickling

A tingling or prickling sensation may occur when touching the skin, applying moisturiser, washing, resting on a pillow, when clothing brushes the skin or when the facial muscles move. This should gradually reduce.

Do not

  • Scratch the skin
  • Rub with a towel
  • Press the skin to test it
  • Attempt to remove the spicules
  • Use tweezers, tape or exfoliation
  • Apply numbing cream without advice
  • Use another spicule treatment to speed it up

Contact Layali Clinic if the sensation is severe or painful, or accompanied by extensive redness, swelling, blistering or a rash.

Peeling & flaking

Peeling or flaking may develop a few days after treatment. If it occurs:

Support natural shedding

  • Allow natural shedding
  • Continue your approved moisturiser
  • Pat skincare on gently
  • Keep the skin clean
  • Protect the skin from the sun
  • Allow extra time before restarting actives

Do not

  • Pick or pull peeling skin
  • Exfoliate
  • Scrub
  • Use an exfoliating glove
  • Use an acid toner
  • Use an enzyme peel
  • Dermaplane
  • Shave aggressively
  • Apply fake tan over peeling skin
Picking or premature exfoliation can increase inflammation, delay healing and raise the risk of pigment change or scarring.

Active skincare

Avoid active skincare for at least 5–7 days, or until the skin is fully settled:

Avoid

  • Retinol
  • Tretinoin
  • Adapalene
  • Prescription retinoids
  • Glycolic, salicylic, lactic and mandelic acid
  • AHAs
  • BHAs
  • Strong vitamin C
  • Benzoyl peroxide
  • Hydroquinone
  • Strong pigment-correctors
  • Exfoliating toner
  • Enzyme peel
  • Facial scrub
  • Home spicule products
  • Strong acne products

When reintroducing your skincare, restart one product at a time.

Sun exposure

For at least 7 days, avoid intentional sun exposure, sunbeds and tanning. Wear a hat, stay in the shade, and use no fake tan until the skin is fully healed.

Use SPF 30+ once tolerated and the skin is intact. Contact the clinic for persistent darkening, lightening or patchy pigmentation.

Exercise & sweating

Avoid strenuous exercise and heavy sweating for approximately 48–72 hours, or until redness, heat and irritation have settled:

Avoid

  • Running
  • Heavy gym sessions
  • HIIT
  • Hot yoga
  • Vigorous cycling
  • Steam-based environments
  • Tight or unclean headwear
  • Exercise causing excessive facial sweating

Gentle walking is usually fine.

Heat, water & swimming

For approximately 72 hours, avoid saunas, steam rooms, hot tubs, swimming pools, sea swimming, very hot showers or baths, heated facial devices, heated masks, sunbeds and excessive heat.

Use cool or lukewarm water.

Sleeping & hygiene

Use a clean pillowcase and avoid sleeping face-down. Keep pets off your pillow and skin, keep hair clean and off the face, and clean your phone. Avoid touching your face with unwashed hands, do not share towels, pillowcases or makeup, and avoid tight masks or headwear rubbing the area.

Facial hair removal

Avoid facial hair removal until the skin is fully settled:

Avoid

  • Waxing
  • Threading
  • Facial razors
  • Dermaplaning
  • Epilation
  • Depilatory creams
  • Laser hair removal
  • Electrolysis

Other facial treatments

Do not have another facial or aesthetic treatment over the area until Layali Clinic has confirmed it has healed:

Avoid until confirmed healed

  • Mechanical microneedling
  • RF microneedling
  • Another bio-microneedling
  • Chemical peels
  • Green Peel
  • Dermaplaning
  • Microdermabrasion
  • Laser
  • IPL
  • Radiofrequency
  • HIFU
  • Titan X
  • Endolaser
  • Facial massage
  • Gua sha
  • Microcurrent
  • Dermal filler
  • Skin boosters
  • Mesotherapy
  • Botulinum toxin
  • PDO threads

Cold sores

Treatments may trigger a cold-sore outbreak in people who carry the herpes simplex virus, especially around the mouth. Contact the clinic promptly for tingling around the lips, burning, itching, small grouped blisters, crusting or a typical outbreak.

Do not

  • Pick the area
  • Apply makeup over active blisters
  • Share lip products, drinks or towels
  • Attend another facial until resolved

Antiviral treatment may be most helpful when started early.

Breakouts & small bumps

Temporary small bumps or blemishes may occur.

Do not

  • Squeeze spots
  • Pick the skin
  • Apply strong acne treatment immediately
  • Use harsh drying products
  • Scrub
  • Apply unapproved spot treatments

Contact us if bumps

  • Become painful
  • Contain pus
  • Spread rapidly
  • Come with extensive redness or swelling
  • Do not settle
  • Become blisters
  • Come with fever or feeling unwell

Infection warning signs

Contact us

Contact Layali Clinic promptly if you experience:

Signs of infection

  • Spreading or worsening redness
  • Increasing warmth
  • Increasing swelling
  • Throbbing or worsening pain
  • Pus or yellow discharge
  • An unpleasant smell
  • An open wound
  • Blistering followed by crusting or discharge
  • Fever
  • Chills
  • Swollen glands
  • Feeling unwell
  • Symptoms that improve then worsen

Do not squeeze or drain the area, apply leftover antibiotics, apply an unprescribed steroid cream, cover it with makeup, continue exfoliating, or delay contacting the clinic.

Excessive irritation, burn or skin injury

Contact us

The following are not normal and need prompt assessment:

Contact Layali Clinic promptly for

  • Intense or increasing burning
  • Severe pain
  • Blistering
  • Open or weeping skin
  • Extensive crusting
  • Raw skin
  • Grey, blue, purple or black skin
  • Significant swelling
  • A rapidly spreading rash
  • Skin peeling in large sheets
  • Severe tenderness
  • Persistent bleeding
  • Signs of scarring

Do not burst blisters, peel damaged skin, apply acids, retinoids or fake tan, use a strong cleanser, cover it with makeup, or apply unprescribed medication.

Pigment changes

Temporary darkening or lightening is possible, especially with sun, inflammation or picking. Contact Layali Clinic for:

Contact us for

  • New dark patches
  • Pale or white areas
  • Uneven pigmentation
  • Worsening pigment change
  • Persistent colour change after healing
  • Pigmentation with blistering or pain

Do not treat pigment change immediately with strong acids, retinoids or bleaching — allow assessment first.

Allergic or hypersensitivity reaction

Call 999

Contact Layali Clinic promptly if you develop:

Contact us promptly for

  • A widespread itchy rash
  • Hives
  • Significant facial swelling
  • Persistent burning
  • Swollen eyelids
  • A reaction extending beyond the treated area

Call 999 immediately for

  • Difficulty breathing
  • Wheezing
  • Swelling of the tongue or throat
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Severe facial swelling
  • Dizziness
  • Collapse
Do not wait when breathing or swallowing is affected.

Eye exposure or eye symptoms

If product enters the eye, rinse immediately with clean lukewarm water, do not rub, remove contact lenses if this is easy to do, and contact Layali Clinic.

Seek urgent advice for persistent eye pain, significant redness, light sensitivity, blurred vision, swelling around the eye, difficulty opening the eye or any change in vision.

When results develop

Immediate redness or swelling is not the result. Improvements develop gradually and results vary by product, spicule concentration, treatment strength, ingredients, skin condition, number of sessions, home skincare, sun exposure and individual response.

Evidence is still developing; we do not guarantee collagen production, pore or scar removal or permanent improvement. Allow the skin to recover before assessing your result.

Repeat treatments

Do not repeat treatment while the skin is red, peeling, tender, inflamed, sunburned, broken, infected, in an acne flare, or during a cold-sore outbreak. The interval between treatments follows the manufacturer instructions, treatment strength, your skin type, your healing and your practitioner's assessment.

Do not use an at-home spicule product between appointments unless approved.

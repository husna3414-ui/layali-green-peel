After Your Treatment

Green Peel & Bio-Microneedling Aftercare

A calm, complete guide to your recovery — what's normal, the peeling phase, and how to protect your beautiful new skin as it renews.

Your skin is now renewing itself. Over the next several days it will go through redness, tightness and — with the Classic formula — visible peeling, before revealing fresher, brighter skin underneath. How you care for it now directly affects your result and, most importantly, protects you from pigmentation.

The golden rules: do not pick or peel the skin off, keep it protected from the sun with SPF 50, and keep it gently cleansed and richly moisturised.

What is completely normal?

Normal & expected

  • Redness (like a warm sunburn) for the first few days
  • Tightness & a "mask-like" feeling
  • Dryness, flaking & peeling over roughly 2–5 days (Classic formula)
  • Skin feeling sensitive or tender
  • Mild swelling
  • Temporary darkening of some areas before they peel and renew
Do NOT pick, pull, rub or exfoliate the peeling skin. Let it shed completely naturally. Picking is the most common cause of scarring and pigmentation after this treatment. If it flakes, let it flake — moisturise and leave it alone.

Sun protection — this is critical

Renewing skin is highly vulnerable to the sun, and sun exposure is the biggest cause of post-treatment pigmentation (darkening). Protecting your skin now is the single most important thing you can do.

Please do

  • Use SPF 50 every single day, even indoors/cloudy
  • Reapply through the day if outdoors
  • Wear a hat & seek shade

Please avoid

  • Direct sun & sunbathing
  • Sunbeds
  • Fake tan on the treated area
  • Sun exposure without SPF — for several weeks

How to care for your skin

Use only — while healing & peeling

  • A gentle, non-foaming cleanser
  • A rich, soothing moisturiser (apply generously & often)
  • SPF 50 every day
  • Any specific products we give you

Avoid for ~1–2 weeks

  • Retinol, retinal & tretinoin
  • Glycolic, lactic & salicylic acids
  • Scrubs & physical exfoliants
  • Strong Vitamin C & other active ingredients

Also, please avoid

  • No makeup for 24–48 hours (let the skin breathe & settle)
  • No touching or rubbing with unwashed hands
  • No picking or peeling the skin

Actives can be reintroduced gradually once your skin has fully renewed and settled — we'll advise you when.

Heat, sweat & activity

While your skin is red and peeling, heat and sweat can irritate it, worsen redness and increase infection risk.

Avoid while peeling / healing

  • Saunas, steam rooms & hot baths
  • Exercise, sweating & hot yoga
  • Swimming pools, hot tubs & the sea
  • Very hot showers on the area

Gentle everyday activity is fine — just avoid getting hot and sweaty until the peeling has finished and your skin has calmed.

Other treatments & wellbeing

Avoid for ~2 weeks

  • Other facials or peels
  • Waxing / hair removal in the area
  • Laser or IPL in the area
  • Aggressive exfoliation

Support your renewal

  • Drink 2–3 litres of water daily
  • Eat protein & fresh fruit/veg
  • Rest well
  • Keep skin moisturised & protected

Your next session & review

The best results come from a course of treatments, spaced a few weeks apart, so your skin can renew and rebuild gradually. Your next session and any review appointment will be arranged as part of your personalised plan. Photographs are often compared at each stage to track your progress.

Between sessions

  • Keep using SPF 50 daily
  • Follow the skincare routine we recommend
  • Avoid sunbeds & excessive sun
  • Come to your next session with clean, bare skin & no active tan

Warning signs — please contact us

Some redness, peeling and sensitivity are normal. Please contact Layali Clinic if you notice:
  • Redness or sensitivity that is worsening rather than settling after several days
  • Increasing rather than easing discomfort
  • A cold sore developing
  • Unusual darkening or patchy pigmentation appearing
  • Any weeping, crusting or a small area that looks infected
  • Anything that simply concerns you

Emergency — seek urgent care

Seek urgent medical care straight away if you experience any signs of a serious reaction or spreading infection, such as:
  • Spreading redness, heat & swelling, with pus or discharge
  • Fever or feeling generally unwell
  • Red streaking spreading from the treated area
  • Facial swelling, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or a widespread rash (signs of a severe allergic reaction)
For a severe reaction or if you feel seriously unwell, call 999 or go to A&E. Then let us know: Layali Clinic 07787 708200.

What is the Green Peel herbal peeling?

The Green Peel is a famous herbal skin-renewal treatment developed by Dr. Schrammek. It is completely natural — a carefully balanced mixture of herbs, algae, enzymes, vitamins and minerals that is massaged into the skin by hand. There are no harsh chemical acids; instead, the finely ground herbs work with a gentle massage to activate the skin's own renewal process.

The herbal actives are absorbed into the skin, boosting circulation and metabolism, and encouraging the skin to shed its tired surface layers and rebuild fresh, healthy skin underneath. The result is skin that looks brighter, feels smoother and appears more even.

And bio-microneedling?

Bio-microneedling uses a device with very fine needles to create thousands of tiny, controlled micro-channels in the skin. Two things happen: your skin responds by producing new collagen and elastin, and those micro-channels let the herbal actives penetrate more deeply and work more effectively.

Together, herbal actives + micro-channels help to

  • Renew and resurface the skin
  • Brighten dull, tired complexions
  • Even out tone and soften pigmentation
  • Refine rough or congested texture
  • Stimulate collagen for firmer, fresher skin
  • Soften the look of fine lines

One treatment, different strengths

The beauty of the Green Peel is that it can be tailored to your skin and to how much downtime you can allow. Different formula strengths exist, from very gentle to deeply renewing:

Lighter formulas — Fresh-Up / Mild

  • A gentle refresh & glow
  • Little to no visible peeling
  • Minimal downtime
  • Lovely before an event or as a "lunchtime" boost
  • Great as a first introduction or in a course

Deeper formula — Classic

  • The original, most intensive renewal
  • Visible flaking & peeling over roughly 2–5 days
  • More noticeable downtime
  • Best for stronger concerns — pigmentation, texture, scarring
  • Plan it around your diary

We'll recommend the right strength for your skin, your goals and your lifestyle at your consultation. The peeling with the Classic formula is completely normal and part of how the treatment works — it is not the skin being "damaged."

Is it right for me?

You may be a good candidate if you have

  • Dull, tired or uneven skin
  • Pigmentation or sun damage
  • Rough or congested texture
  • Early fine lines
  • Acne scarring (once acne is under control)
  • Good general health & realistic expectations

It may NOT be suitable if you

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active skin infection, active acne breakout, cold sore or eczema/rosacea flare in the area
  • Have used isotretinoin (Roaccutane) recently
  • Have sunburn or an active/fresh tan
  • Have a history of keloid scarring
  • Are prone to pigmentation and can't commit to strict sun protection

A consultation and, where appropriate, a patch test will determine suitability. Treatment may be declined if it is not safe or appropriate for your skin.

Realistic results & why a course works best

Many clients notice their skin looks brighter and feels smoother after a single treatment, especially with a lighter formula. However, the most rewarding results come from a course of treatments — because the skin renews and rebuilds gradually.

A typical plan

  • A short course of sessions spaced a few weeks apart
  • Deeper concerns (pigmentation, texture, scarring) benefit from more sessions
  • Your personalised plan is agreed at your consultation

When will I see results?

With the Classic formula, the "reveal" comes once the peeling has finished — usually within about a week — when fresh, brighter skin is uncovered. With lighter formulas you'll see an immediate glow. Improvements in tone and texture continue to build over the following weeks as collagen develops.

How long do results last?

Results can last for months, but skin ages and is affected by sun, lifestyle and hormones. Maintenance treatments — and daily SPF and good skincare — help you keep your glow long-term. We'll recommend a maintenance rhythm that suits you.

What's the downtime?

Downtime depends on the formula strength. This is completely normal and part of how the treatment renews your skin.

What to expect — especially with the Classic formula

  • Redness (like a warm sunburn) on the day and for a few days
  • Tightness and a "mask-like" feeling for the first day or two
  • Visible flaking and peeling over roughly 2–5 days
  • Sensitivity for several days
  • Do not pick or peel the skin off — let it shed naturally

Lighter formulas involve far less (often no visible) peeling. We'll always tell you honestly what to expect so you can plan around events, work and holidays.

Side effects & risks

The Green Peel with bio-microneedling is generally very well tolerated, but every skin treatment carries some risk. We explain everything fully at your consultation.

Common & temporary

  • Redness
  • Peeling & flaking
  • Tightness & dryness
  • Sensitivity
  • Mild swelling
  • Temporary darkening before the skin renews

Less common / important risks

  • Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (darkening) — the most important risk, especially in darker skin
  • Infection
  • Prolonged redness or sensitivity
  • Cold-sore (herpes) reactivation
  • Scarring — usually only if skin is picked

Pigmentation — please read

Because this treatment renews the skin, there is a risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (temporary or, rarely, longer-lasting darkening). This risk is higher in darker skin tones (Fitzpatrick IV–VI). It is why strict sun avoidance and daily SPF 50 afterwards are so important, and why we may recommend a patch test or a gentler formula for your skin. We assess this carefully with you before treating.

When we can't treat you (contraindications)

For your safety, we won't proceed — or we'll postpone — if any of the following apply:

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Active infection, active acne breakout, cold sore or open/broken skin in the area
  • Active eczema, dermatitis or a rosacea flare in the area
  • Recent use of isotretinoin (Roaccutane)
  • Sunburn or a fresh/active tan (including recent fake tan)
  • History of keloid scarring
  • Certain medical conditions or medications until reviewed

This is not a complete list — your full history is reviewed on your consent form and at your appointment.

Alternatives

The Green Peel isn't the only way to improve skin quality. Depending on your skin and goals, alternatives include:

  • Chemical peels (acid-based, various strengths)
  • Microneedling on its own
  • A medical-grade skincare routine (e.g. targeted actives & SPF)
  • Doing nothing

We'll always give you honest advice about which option best suits your skin — including if another treatment (or none at all) would serve you better.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really all natural?

Yes — the Green Peel herbal mixture is made from herbs, algae, enzymes, vitamins and minerals rather than chemical acids. Combined with bio-microneedling, it renews the skin naturally.

Does it hurt?

The herbal massage feels warm and tingly. Bio-microneedling can feel like light scratching or prickling; we keep you comfortable and can use a numbing cream where appropriate.

Will my skin peel?

With the deeper Classic formula, yes — visible flaking and peeling over roughly 2–5 days is normal and part of the renewal. Lighter formulas peel far less, or not visibly at all.

How long is the downtime?

Redness and tightness for a few days, and peeling for around 2–5 days with the Classic formula. Please don't pick the skin — let it shed naturally.

When will I see results?

You'll see fresher, brighter skin once peeling finishes (about a week with Classic), and continued improvement in tone and texture over the following weeks. A course gives the best results.

Is it safe for darker skin?

It can be, but darker skin (Fitzpatrick IV–VI) has a higher risk of temporary darkening (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation). We assess this carefully, may recommend a gentler formula and/or a patch test, and strict sun protection afterwards is essential.

Can I have it on my neck or body?

Yes — the face, neck, décolletage, hands, back and other body areas can be treated, subject to assessment.

What if I decide it's not for me?

There's absolutely no pressure. A consultation is simply a chance to get expert advice and your questions answered — you're never obliged to book.

Before Your Treatment

Preparing for Green Peel & Bio-Microneedling

A little preparation makes a real difference to how well your skin renews, how comfortable you are, and your final result.

This treatment works by encouraging your skin to renew itself, so calm, healthy, well-prepared skin gives the best and safest result. Please follow this guide in the days leading up to your appointment.

Good preparation may help to

  • Reduce irritation & sensitivity
  • Lower the risk of complications
  • Lower the risk of pigmentation
  • Support even, healthy renewal
  • Optimise your final result
Important: never stop prescribed medication without first speaking to your GP or prescriber. If you're unsure about any advice below, please contact us before your appointment.
◆

1–2 weeks before

Avoid other treatments in the same area

Why: your skin needs to be calm and fully recovered. Recent treatments can leave skin sensitised and increase irritation and complication risk.

Please avoid for ~1–2 weeks before

  • Other facials or peels
  • Waxing in the area
  • Laser or IPL
  • Microneedling elsewhere in the area
  • Threading or epilation in the area
  • Aggressive exfoliating facials

If you've recently had any treatment in the area, please let us know.

Protect your skin from the sun — and come with no active tan

Why: sun-exposed, tanned or recently fake-tanned skin is far more likely to develop pigmentation (darkening) after treatment. This is the single most important thing you can do to protect your result.

Please avoid

  • Sunbeds
  • Sunburn
  • Excessive sun exposure
  • Fake tan / self-tan
  • Arriving with a fresh or active tan

Cold sores

If you are prone to cold sores, please tell us before your appointment — preventative antiviral medication may be recommended, as skin renewal can otherwise trigger an outbreak.

◆

5–7 days before

Pause active skincare

Why: active ingredients make skin more sensitive and reactive, which can increase irritation and complication risk during renewal.

Temporarily stop ~5–7 days before

  • Retinol, retinal & tretinoin
  • Glycolic, lactic & salicylic acid (AHAs/BHAs)
  • Vitamin C in strong/exfoliating forms
  • Scrubs & physical exfoliants
  • Any other strong active products

Blood thinners — only if needling may draw blood

Why: bio-microneedling can cause pinpoint bleeding. Blood-thinning medicines and supplements can increase this and cause more bruising. This only matters where the needling depth is likely to draw blood.

Where medically appropriate, and only with your doctor's approval, you may reduce

  • Aspirin, ibuprofen & other anti-inflammatories
  • Fish oil / Omega-3, Vitamin E, ginkgo, high-dose garlic/ginger, turmeric supplements
Never stop prescribed blood-thinning medication (such as warfarin, apixaban, rivaroxaban or clopidogrel) without your doctor's approval. Please just tell us — we will adapt the treatment to keep you safe.
◆

48 hours before

Please avoid

  • Alcohol (increases sensitivity & bruising)
  • Excessive salt (encourages swelling)
  • Waxing / hair removal in the area

Please do

  • Hydrate well — aim for 2–3 litres of water daily (unless medically advised otherwise)
  • Prioritise good sleep to support healing
  • Eat well — protein & fresh fruit/veg support renewal

Well-hydrated, well-rested skin tolerates renewal better and heals more comfortably.

◆

On the day of treatment

Please do

  • Arrive with clean, bare skin — no makeup
  • Come with no active tan
  • Eat a proper meal & drink water beforehand
  • Wear comfortable clothing (easy to protect the treated area)
  • Allow extra time so you arrive relaxed

Please avoid

  • Makeup, SPF, serums & heavy moisturisers on the area
  • Fake tan
  • Waxing or exfoliating the area that day
  • Strenuous exercise beforehand
◆

Plan your peeling & downtime

With the deeper Classic formula, your skin will be red, then flaky and peeling for roughly 2–5 days. Please plan this around your diary so you feel comfortable and confident.

Best to avoid soon after (especially with Classic)

  • Important events & weddings
  • Professional photography
  • Holidays with sun exposure
  • Big work presentations

If you have a lighter formula, downtime is minimal — but we'll still confirm what to expect at your consultation so you can plan.

◆

Patch test & sensitive skin

Where appropriate — particularly for reactive skin, allergy history, or darker skin tones with a higher pigmentation risk — we may carry out a patch test before your full treatment. This helps us confirm the right formula and keep you safe. Please tell us about any allergies, including to herbs, plants, aspirin or salicylates.

Please contact us to postpone if you

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active acne breakout, cold sore, infection or open/broken skin in the area
  • Have an eczema, dermatitis or rosacea flare in the area
  • Have sunburn or a fresh/active tan
  • Have recently used isotretinoin (Roaccutane)
  • Feel unwell or have a fever
  • Have started new medication or another aesthetic/skin treatment
If in doubt, always contact us first — in many cases we can simply reschedule to a safer time.

Our priority is your safety

If we believe treatment is not in your best interests on the day, we may postpone it until it can be performed safely. Your safety will always come before proceeding with treatment.
